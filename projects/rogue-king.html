The Hybrid Core Rules

Three mechanical pillars that rethink traditional chess boundaries within the prototype scope:

Pillar 01

Piece Combat & HP

Pieces no longer capture in a single hit. Each piece carries tailored HP and Attack points. Pawns skirmish; Queens act as high-damage battlefield bosses.

Pillar 02

Deck-Building Action Cards

Draw cards every turn to temporarily alter board physics: grant a Knight diagonal bishop leaps, place barrier tiles, or trigger teleportation.

Pillar 03

The Vulnerable Monarch

Checkmate is replaced with King execution. The King can wield special defensive spells but remains the ultimate objective for board wipe.

Playable Screens & Systems

High-fidelity interactive states captured directly from the JavaScript & HTML5 game engine:

Main Menu & Navigation

1. Gothic Title & Campaign Hub

Gothic title hub providing seamless access to the Story Campaign, Quick Skirmish mode, and the custom Deck Builder interface.

Tactical Playfield

2. 8x8 Chessboard Arena

Full chess board featuring real-time piece HP bars, live hand of action cards, discard piles, and an active piece inspector panel.

Combat Clashes

3. Piece HP Duel Overlay

Direct clash interface initiating when pieces engage. Displays character portraits, comparative attack/defense stats, and action commands.

Strategic Drafting

4. Dark Magic Card Library

Card drafting and selection interface where players formulate synergy-focused 20-card tactical decks from dark magic disciplines.

Narrative Lore

5. Story & World Lore

Gothic narrative modal detailing the centuries-old lore of the Rouge Arena, forbidden blood magic, and the war against the Ebon Legion.

Match Resolution

6. Victory & King Execution

End-game resolution sequence triggered when the enemy commander is slain, rewarding players with tactical victory.
